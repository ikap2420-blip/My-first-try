About Us

LogoLoomShopArt is a logo design and branding studio. We design logos and brand identities that fit your business, your audience and your needs.

Design philosophy
A good logo is simple, clear and works everywhere, from a phone screen to a signboard.
Vision
To help every business, big or small, look professional and be remembered.
Mission
Understand each client's story, turn it into a thoughtful design, and deliver files that are ready to use.
